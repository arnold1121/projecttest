A STORY FROM THE SHORE

From the southern coast
to your table.

SeaFishPick takes inspiration from the everyday magic of coastal kitchens — the catch of the day, the aroma of spices blooming in oil, and recipes shared across generations.

Our brand story follows a simple thread: Kanyakumari’s coastline, fishing communities around Colachel, and the familiar comfort of a well-loved pickle jar on the family table.
